# JM1 Public Brand & Content Standard v1

Status: CANON-CANDIDATE — PENDING FOUNDER & CEO APPROVAL

## Core Rules

1. Human-First.
2. Why-First.
3. People before systems.
4. Needs before architecture.
5. Outcomes before implementation.
6. Plain language before enterprise jargon.
7. Public content explains benefits, not internal mechanics.
8. Division pathways remain obvious.
9. Technical sophistication should be experienced, not explained.
10. No founder-story dependency.
11. No invented personal narrative.
12. Infrastructure belongs primarily below the public surface.
13. Every public section must answer at least one: why does this matter, who does this help, how can we help, or where should I go next?

## Tone

Allowed: warm, confident, clear, modern, sophisticated, human, concise, mission-driven, and enterprise-capable without enterprise jargon.

Avoid: cold, technical, architecture-first, self-impressed, overexplained, AI-centric, or jargon-heavy.

Human-first does not mean simplistic. The public standard is modern enterprise confidence with human clarity.
