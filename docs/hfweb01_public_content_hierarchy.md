# HFWEB-01 Public Content Hierarchy

Status: Applied

1. WHY: Why does JM1 exist?
2. PEOPLE: Who is JM1 here to serve?
3. NEED: What is the visitor trying to accomplish?
4. HELP: Which J Merrill One company can help?
5. PROOF: What evidence shows JM1 can help?
6. ACTION: What should the visitor do next?

Internal system explanation must not outrank any of these.

## Applied Surfaces

Homepage now leads with `What you build should last`, followed by human need pathways, Why One continuity, four companies, proof, and contact.

`/why-we-exist` now carries the least technical public expression: belief, human stakes, four company expressions, and Start Here.

`/contact` uses plain language around need and team follow-up while preserving the governed BP-09 intake path.

Secondary enterprise pages are footer-accessible only and cannot interrupt the primary client journey.
