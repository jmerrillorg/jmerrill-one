# JM1 Public Tree Model v1

Status: CANON — v1.0

## Trunk

J Merrill One.

Public expression: parent identity, connection, continuity, stewardship, and shared commitment.

## Branches

- J Merrill Publishing
- J Merrill Financial
- J Merrill Foundation
- J Merrill Productions

The branches are the four operating companies people can recognize and choose from.

## Leaves / Fruit

Services, outcomes, impact, and client value: published books, protected families, community programs, donor/volunteer participation, productions work, and messages carried further.

## Roots

Microsoft ecosystem, Dataverse, Dynamics 365, Azure, Power Platform, Copilot Studio, AI, automation, governance, and infrastructure.

## Public Rule

Roots make the experience possible; roots are not the experience.

Roots are discussed publicly only when the audience has a legitimate reason to care about the infrastructure itself: government/procurement, technology partner, institutional partner, due diligence, security review, or vendor integration. Roots do not belong in average client navigation.
