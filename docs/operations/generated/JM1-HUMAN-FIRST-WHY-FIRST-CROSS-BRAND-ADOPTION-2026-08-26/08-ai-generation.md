# AI Generation Guard Evidence

Last verified: 2026-08-26T15:57:26Z

## Standard

AI-generated human-facing output must be evaluated by the same branch overlay before send or publish.

The policy blocks:

- wrong recipient or relationship;
- wrong brand sender or voice;
- internal platform language;
- raw placeholders;
- GUIDs, hashes, checksums, and execution IDs;
- high-risk legal, financial, contractual, rights, pastoral, sensitive, or public-statement output without human review.

## Branch Coverage

Financial: policy gate bound into existing communication evaluation path.

Foundation: pre-publish guard tests cover donor, applicant, volunteer, fundraising, sender, and internal-language cases.

Productions: pre-publish guard tests cover talent, vendor, client, rights, sender, and internal-language cases.

AIC: pre-publish guard tests cover member, volunteer, ministry, pastoral-sensitive, sender, and internal-platform cases.
