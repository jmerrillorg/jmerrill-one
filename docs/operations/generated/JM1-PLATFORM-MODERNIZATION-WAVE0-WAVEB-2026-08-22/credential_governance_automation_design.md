# Credential Governance Automation Design

Status: DESIGN_ONLY

Minimum viable automation:

1. Daily Entra app credential inventory.
2. Normalize expiry, app owner, service principal, RBAC, federated credential, and known consumer settings.
3. Classify P0/P1/P2 by expiry and consumer criticality.
4. Emit pull-request evidence into One repo generated operations artifacts.
5. Notify owner without exposing secret values.

No automation was deployed in this package.
