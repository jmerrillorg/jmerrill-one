# Sintra Projection Refresh Governance

Status: POLICY-CANDIDATE

For any future Sintra projection:

- The authoritative source remains primary.
- The projection owner must be named.
- The projection must cite the source path, artifact, table, or public URL.
- Review is triggered by source changes, policy changes, workflow changes, public-site changes, or Founder direction.
- Source-change refresh must update or retire the projection before use.
- Stale projections must be disabled, removed from active use, or clearly marked non-authoritative when a safe mechanism exists.
- No silent canon drift is allowed.

Sintra projections are assistive references only. They do not become record owners, systems of record, or canonical policy authority.
