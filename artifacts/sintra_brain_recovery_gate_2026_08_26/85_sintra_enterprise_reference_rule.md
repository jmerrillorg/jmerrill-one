# Sintra Enterprise Reference Rule

J Merrill One may retain high-level enterprise reference knowledge when it helps umbrella reasoning, cross-brand orientation, routing, and shared governance.

## Appropriate In One

- What each division does.
- Canonical public identity and approved brand relationship.
- High-level service or program scope.
- Enterprise relationship between One, Publishing, Financial, Foundation, and any separately confirmed branch.
- Shared governance, routing, approval, and authority-boundary rules.
- Pointers to authoritative external systems, repositories, websites, Dataverse, SharePoint, or branch workspaces.

## Not Appropriate As One Brain Authority

- Publishing editorial workflow, manuscript-stage operating rules, author-specific operational detail, style-sheet governance, and book/project execution instructions.
- Financial client workflow, compliance-sensitive operating procedures, detailed service delivery rules, or regulated product guidance beyond approved enterprise reference.
- Foundation participant, donor, program, or beneficiary operations.
- Brand-specific execution instructions that could drift from the authoritative source if duplicated.

A One-level record saying a branch exists is not contamination by itself. A One-level record that governs how a branch executes its operations is generally cross-workspace scope risk unless Founder governance explicitly makes it enterprise authority.
