# Sintra Brain AI Admission Policy

Status: CANON-CANDIDATE

Before any item enters a production Sintra Brain AI workspace, answer yes to all seven tests:

1. Current today.
2. Canonical or approved current reference.
3. Owned by the correct enterprise or division authority.
4. Needed by the specific workspace.
5. Marketing-safe.
6. Not superseded.
7. Traceable to source/version/authority.

If any answer is missing or unresolved, classify the item as QUARANTINE / UNKNOWN_AUTHORITY and do not load it into production Brain AI.

Durable Brain AI may contain evergreen public/marketing-safe truth. Campaign context belongs in temporary campaign instructions. Chat context remains disposable working material and must never automatically become Brain AI.

No destructive cleanup is authorized by this policy.
