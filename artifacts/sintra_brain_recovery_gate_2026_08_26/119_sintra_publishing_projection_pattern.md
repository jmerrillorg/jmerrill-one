# Sintra Publishing Projection Pattern

Preferred model:

```text
Governed Publishing source
        |
        v
minimal Sintra Publishing projection/reference
        |
        v
One retains only high-level enterprise awareness
```

## Use

Use this only after the governed Publishing source is certified. The Sintra Publishing projection should be a pointer, short source-backed summary, or scoped drafting aid with provenance.

## Avoid

- Full operational canon copied into One.
- Duplicated authoritative documents.
- Manual copy/paste with no provenance.
- Sintra becoming the record owner.
- Treating existing J Merrill One Brain content as Publishing source authority.

J Merrill One may retain only routing, brand identity, or high-level enterprise awareness where actually needed.
