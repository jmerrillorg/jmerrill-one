# Sintra Adoption Recommendation v2

Recommendation: LIMITED LAB ADOPTION — HIGH GOVERNANCE OVERHEAD.

Sintra proved useful for read-only boundary reasoning, especially unknown/unseen record handling and M365/Dataverse authority separation. It also showed moderate governance overhead: Publishing records inside One Brain were treated as a governing rulebook, and Financial scope was only partially represented.

The evidence still shows meaningful overlap with Microsoft-first systems. Inbox Manager was disabled after live dependency check. Social Media Manager and Daily Summarizer may justify only narrow lab testing if they stay non-public and subordinate to Social Recovery and M365 governance.

Adoption should be decided from lab response quality, action safety, Microsoft-first fit, and source authority awareness, not from feature count.
