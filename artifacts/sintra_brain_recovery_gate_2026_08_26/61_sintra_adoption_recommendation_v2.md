# Sintra Adoption Recommendation v2

Recommendation: LIMITED LAB ADOPTION - HIGH GOVERNANCE OVERHEAD.

Sintra proved useful for read-only boundary reasoning, especially unknown/unseen record handling and M365/Dataverse authority separation. It also showed moderate governance overhead: Publishing records inside the inspected J Merrill One Brain were treated as a governing rulebook, and Financial scope was only partially represented.

Founder-provided workspace switcher evidence improves Sintra's potential value because Sintra has a native organizational boundary mechanism: One, Publishing, Financial, and Foundation can be separated by workspace. That value only matters if Brain content follows source authority and does not duplicate branch operations into One.

The evidence still shows meaningful overlap with Microsoft-first systems. Inbox Manager was disabled after live dependency check and remains off. Social Media Manager and Daily Summarizer may justify only narrow lab testing if they stay non-public and subordinate to Social Recovery and M365 governance.

The final mutation plan does not upgrade adoption. It separates cleanup readiness from product adoption and places Brain mutations on hold because no safe reversible quarantine primitive is proven.

After this approved-automation pass, Sintra remains useful enough to retain only as a read-only/drafting assistant: YES - LIMITED VALUE. The value is in internal ideation, read-only analysis, internal drafting, non-authoritative summaries, and shadow comparison. It is not enough to justify autonomous public action, inbox operation, or source-of-record status.

Adoption should be decided from lab response quality, action safety, Microsoft-first fit, and source authority awareness, not from feature count.
