# Sintra Adoption Recommendation v2

Recommendation: LIMITED LAB ADOPTION — HIGH GOVERNANCE OVERHEAD.

Sintra remains potentially useful for ideation, draft support, supplemental summaries, and read-only Brain Q&A after the Brain is cleaned or bounded. It is not ready for production authority, public posting, commenting, email action, connector scope changes, or canonical enterprise memory.

The evidence still shows meaningful overlap with Microsoft-first systems. Inbox Manager is the clearest disable candidate. Social Media Manager and Daily Summarizer may justify narrow lab testing only if they stay non-public and subordinate to Social Recovery and M365 governance.

Adoption should be decided from lab response quality, action safety, Microsoft-first fit, and source authority awareness, not from feature count.
