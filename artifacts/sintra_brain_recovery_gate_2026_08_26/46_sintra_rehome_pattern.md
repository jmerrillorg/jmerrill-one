## Application

Rehome does not mean Sintra becomes the new system of record. For Publishing, Financial, Foundation, and enterprise One records, the authoritative source remains the governed repo, Dataverse, SharePoint, M365, brand workspace, or approved public web canon.

J Merrill Publishing, J Merrill Financial, and J Merrill Foundation are now confirmed Sintra workspaces, but their existence does not make them authoritative operational sources by default. They may consume or project governed authority.

If Sintra supports safe workspace separation, the better pattern is usually a curated projection or reference copy with explicit source provenance. Physical movement is appropriate only when it is reversible, source-backed, and separately approved.

J Merrill Productions workspace is not confirmed in current evidence; do not propose moving Productions knowledge into a nonexistent Sintra workspace.
