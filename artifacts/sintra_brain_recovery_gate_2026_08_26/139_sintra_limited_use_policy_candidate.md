# Sintra Limited Use Policy Candidate

Status: POLICY-CANDIDATE

## Permitted

- Read-only internal analysis.
- Ideation.
- Internal drafts.
- Non-authoritative summaries.
- Shadow comparison.

## Not Permitted

- Canonical authority.
- Direct social publishing.
- Public commenting.
- Autonomous email.
- Customer/client outreach.
- M365, Dataverse, or Azure writes.
- Cross-brand operational authority.

## Brain Trust Rule

Brain content is advisory only unless independently source-certified. Legacy Brain records in J Merrill One must not override Founder authority, repository canon, Dataverse, Microsoft 365, SharePoint, governed websites, or brand-owned source systems.
