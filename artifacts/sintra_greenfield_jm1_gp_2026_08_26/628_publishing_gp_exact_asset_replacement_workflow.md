# Exact Asset Replacement Workflow

1. Produce or select the local final creative.
2. Validate official logo presence and approved dimensions.
3. Upload the exact local file through a surface that preserves file fidelity.
4. Apply platform-specific Facebook, Instagram, or LinkedIn post details.
5. Verify destination, date, time, caption, creative, and public preview.
6. Remove or move the old weak scheduled version only after the replacement is verified.

Soshie scheduled-post media replacement remains disallowed where it transforms or regenerates the image instead of binding the exact approved file.
