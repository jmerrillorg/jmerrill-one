# GP Phase 2 Founder Packet

Is Sintra worth keeping? Not for the approved Phase 2 job as tested.

Exact job tested: connectorless, helperless, automation-free executive thinking room for J Merrill One - GP.

What worked: the four default automations were successfully disabled and stayed off after reload. The workspace remained one Brain record, zero connectors, zero hired helpers, zero automations on, and zero external actions.

What failed: Sintra auto-delegated an executive opportunity evaluation to Buddy, then used helper personas as collaborators/owners even after explicit no-helper instruction. This means the helperless executive-room role was not proven.

One-record Brain sufficient? Not determinable. The failure looked less like missing knowledge and more like product/reasoning boundary failure.

Need connectors? No.

Need helpers? No. Helpers are the problem in this test, not the solution.

Need automations? No.

Need branch workspaces? No.

Need another technical phase? No. Stop the pilot unless Sintra can offer a reliable no-helper/no-delegation mode.

Should Jackie begin a real-usage pilot? No.
