# Sintra Zero-Leakage Standard

Classification: SINTRA BRAND-ISOLATED SOCIAL ARCHITECTURE

A branch Brain may contain only branch-owned operational knowledge plus the minimum enterprise identity needed to understand its relationship to J Merrill One.

## Publishing Brain

May know J Merrill Publishing identity, authors, titles, publishing services, editorial/production/distribution context, Publishing social rules, and Publishing brand standards.

May not contain Financial client or planning operations, Foundation participant or donor operations, Productions project operations, legacy recovery Brain material, or One enterprise operational detail beyond the minimal relationship reference.

## Other Branches

Financial, Foundation, and Productions Brains follow the same isolation rule: branch-owned knowledge only, no author/title catalog unless a future approved cross-brand project explicitly requires a narrow public summary.
