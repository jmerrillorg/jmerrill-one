The public creative quality gate requires people-first hooks, why-first captions, official-logo visual binding for every new image, and removal of internal governance language from public posts before any schedule is treated as marketing-ready.

Exact-file scheduling rule: if a Sintra/Soshie scheduled-post media workflow cannot preserve the exact approved local asset, the post must be recreated through an exact-file-preserving platform surface. Facebook and Instagram may carry platform-specific post details inside the same Meta flow when that surface can be verified; otherwise separate platform drafts are permitted. A weak scheduled version is removed only after the replacement post has verified destination, date, time, caption, and creative binding.
