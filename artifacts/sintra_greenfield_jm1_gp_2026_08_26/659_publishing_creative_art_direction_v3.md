# 659 Publishing Creative Art Direction v3

Date: 2026-08-28
Brand: J Merrill Publishing
Status: ACTIVE

## Creative Standard

J Merrill Publishing public creative must feel editorial, literary, imaginative, polished, warm, contemporary, and distinctive. It should look like a creative publishing company presenting books, authors, ideas, and reader moments with care.

The official tagline remains approved:

```text
Helping Authors Help Themselves
```

Every public image must include the exact official J Merrill logo somewhere within the image. Do not generate, redraw, approximate, stylize, or transform the logo.

## Required Public Feel

- Editorial composition
- Human reason to care
- Actual book cover when title-specific
- Actual author portrait where available and permitted
- Strong hierarchy
- Warm literary texture
- Brand presence without making the image look like a database record

## Rejected Public Feel

- Registry cards
- Date cards
- Content-category cards
- Operational footer language
- Campaign metadata
- Generic automation-card layout
- Corporate report styling
- Governance vocabulary

## Public Language Prohibitions

Do not place these phrases on public images or captions unless the public subject is legitimately about that term:

- 30-Day Social Activation
- rights-safe
- official-logo publishing post
- source-safe
- uncleared excerpts
- governed
- content pillar
- validation

## People-First / Why-First Rule

Internal planning may ask: why should somebody care?

Public creative must translate that answer into a human-facing message. Do not put internal reasoning, validation language, or compliance labels onto the image.

## Source Asset Rule

For title content, the actual book cover should normally be the hero asset.

For author content, use a real author portrait, real book cover, or editorial composition where available and permitted.

Never generate fake:

- Book covers
- Author likenesses
- Logos

## Scheduler Implication

A scheduler is acceptable only if it preserves the exact approved creative file and provides visible destination/date/time/media proof. If a scheduler substitutes or regenerates the image, it cannot be the final publisher for that campaign.
