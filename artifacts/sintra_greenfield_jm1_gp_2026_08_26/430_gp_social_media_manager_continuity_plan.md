# Social Media Manager Continuity Plan

After September 25, Social Media Manager should operate as a governed scheduling system, not as an autonomous brand authority.

## Rolling Content Horizon

Maintain a 30-day strategic horizon, a 14-day draft queue, and a 7-day approved execution queue.

## Content Pillars

Use the evergreen pillars in `420_gp_one_evergreen_social_cadence.json`.

## Approval Queue

Keep routine posts, Founder-review posts, and functional/compliance-review posts separated so review effort matches risk.

## Creative Queue

Prepare creative in batches, with variation across typography-led graphics, people-centered imagery, brand-family cards, quote graphics, and simple conceptual visuals.

## Scheduling Queue

Schedule approved posts to certified One destinations only:

- Facebook: J Merrill One
- Instagram: @jmerrillone
- LinkedIn Organization: J Merrill One

## Published State

After publication, verify destination, copy, creative, link, formatting, and absence of automated engagement.

## Performance Feedback

Use reach, engagement, clicks, saves, shares, inquiries, development effort, and revision burden to shape the next cycle.

## Next-Cycle Generation

Generate the next 7-14 days before the queue runs empty. Use real enterprise movement through approved summaries, not raw sensitive records.
