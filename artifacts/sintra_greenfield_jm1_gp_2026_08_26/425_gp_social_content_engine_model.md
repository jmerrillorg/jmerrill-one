# Social Content Engine Model

```text
Enterprise/brand activity
        ↓
Content opportunity
        ↓
Correct pillar
        ↓
Correct brand owner
        ↓
Sintra/helper ideation
        ↓
Platform-native variants
        ↓
Creative
        ↓
Review/approval
        ↓
Schedule
        ↓
Publish
        ↓
Performance evidence
        ↓
Next content decisions
```

The engine should convert real JM1 movement into useful public content without giving helpers brand ownership, ingesting sensitive records unnecessarily, or treating daily posting as the purpose.
