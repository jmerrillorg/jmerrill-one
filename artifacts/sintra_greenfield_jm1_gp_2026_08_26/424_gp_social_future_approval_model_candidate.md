# Social Future Approval Model Candidate

This is design only. It does not remove Founder approval authority during the active pilot.

## Routine / Pre-Approved Pillar

Routine posts inside established content standards may eventually move through a lighter review path once evidence shows the standards are stable.

## Founder Review

Required for Founder voice or quotes, major enterprise announcements, sensitive subjects, cross-brand policy claims, new campaigns, and potentially controversial topics.

## Functional / Compliance Review

Required where Financial, privacy, rights, legal, regulated, or other specialized concerns apply.

## Current Pilot Rule

The first-week Founder approval remains appropriate for calibration. Any future reduction in review burden must be evidence-based and explicitly approved.
