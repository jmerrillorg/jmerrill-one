# One Evergreen Social Cadence

The initial 30-day J Merrill One calendar is an activation calendar, not the end of the social program.

## Continuing Pillars

1. One / Enterprise Story — what JM1 is, how the brands relate, and what One coordinates.
2. People-First — how JM1 designs services, systems, and experiences around people.
3. Why-First — purpose before process, technology, automation, or offer.
4. Publishing — authors, books, publishing journey, stories, launches.
5. Financial — estate planning, advanced planning, funeral/pre-need planning, and planning education.
6. Foundation — community, literacy, programs, partnerships, giving, impact.
7. Productions — storytelling, media, production process, creative work.
8. Founder Perspective — leadership, building JM1, entrepreneurship, lessons, decisions.
9. Behind the Build — systems, technology, AI, Microsoft-first architecture, operations.
10. Community / Conversation — questions, observations, useful prompts, audience participation.

## Operating Standard

Use a sustainable content architecture instead of inventing hundreds of unrelated posts. Real enterprise movement should increasingly feed the queue through approved summaries, not raw sensitive records.
