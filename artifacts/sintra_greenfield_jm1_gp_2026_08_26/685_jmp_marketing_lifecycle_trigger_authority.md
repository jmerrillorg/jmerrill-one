# JMP Marketing Lifecycle Trigger Authority

Dataverse and governed Publishing records decide why and when lifecycle marketing is eligible. Sintra, Cody, and other AI surfaces may help draft strategy, captions, visual concepts, or calendar options, but they do not certify that a title is cover-approved, distribution-live, release-ready, or consent-eligible.

The current source-backed intake table is `jm1_publishingintake`, with activation still blocked pending entity-set, app-user/security-role, adapter, and end-to-end write validation. The current title lifecycle architecture is canon-candidate and keeps client-title automation frozen. The event-driven runtime gap register already names marketing triggers, but most are not production-proven.

Required proof for every lifecycle marketing trigger: source record, source field or event, timestamp, actor/system, idempotency key, approval/rights state, suppression/consent state where communication is involved, and a clear public-ready outcome.

No public-facing marketing copy should mention internal trigger names, Dataverse, governance packages, or automation machinery.
