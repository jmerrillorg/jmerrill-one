# Publishing Social Rights Gate

Safe by default: owned cover, author/title identity, official descriptions, JMP-created graphics, rights-cleared images, paraphrased themes, and public links to official Publishing pages.

Do not assume rights to lengthy excerpts, third-party reviews, copyrighted photography, audiobook clips, third-party logos/media, private manuscripts, or private author records.

Unclear excerpt rights do not block title marketing. Use a title spotlight, author spotlight, topic/theme, reader question, why-it-matters angle, or official description instead.

Every new creative image must include the approved shared J Merrill logo somewhere within the image.
