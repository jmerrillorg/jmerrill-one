# JM1 Customer Feedback Standard v1

Status: CANON - v1.0

Founder & CEO approval recorded by CV-01A on 2026-08-13T03:16:56Z.

## Purpose

Customer Voice is JM1's default Microsoft-native feedback platform where survey/feedback semantics fit.

## Ownership

J Merrill One owns the shared Customer Voice capability, feedback governance, reusable survey baseline, privacy/security boundaries, Dataverse integration standard, enterprise metric vocabulary, and repository handoff contracts. Division repositories own division survey content, branding, website placement, and operational follow-up.

## Project Strategy

Use distinct division-aligned Customer Voice projects/surveys: Publishing Author Experience, Financial Client Experience, Foundation Community Experience, and Productions Project Experience. Jackie Smith Jr. Coaching Experience is defined for future use only and is not activated until the coaching offering launches.

## Core Questions

The shared core measures overall satisfaction, feeling heard/understood, communication, clarity, professionalism, likelihood to recommend, and open feedback. Division questions may customize the experience without creating long corporate questionnaires or requesting unnecessary sensitive information.

## Privacy

Enterprise Contact identity may be shared, but response content follows the division business context. Financial, Foundation recipient, Publishing author, and Productions unreleased-project feedback require bounded access.

## Testimonial Permission

Feedback submission is not testimonial consent. An optional permission-to-contact question may be included. Actual testimonial publication requires separate approval evidence.

## Website Pattern

The public pattern is `Share Your Experience`, normally as a link/button in a footer or client resources area. The website must not expose Customer Voice jargon.

## Automation

Public feedback links may launch before automated invitations. Future invitations may use Customer Voice, Dataverse, and Power Automate triggers where appropriate.

## Service Recovery

Low or serious feedback routes to division review. Create Cases only when service-resolution semantics fit; do not send automatic consequential responses.

## Forms Boundary

Microsoft Forms remains valid for internal, registration, simple operational, and non-feedback-semantics forms. Do not migrate every Form into Customer Voice.

## Journeys Boundary

Customer Voice owns feedback. Customer Insights Journeys may later orchestrate invitations, but Customer Voice activation does not depend on Journeys.

## Metrics

Use response count, response rate where invitation-based, satisfaction, recommendation, communication, clarity, recurring themes, and service-recovery count. Do not invent historical values.

## Repository Rule

Enterprise standards live in One. Division implementation lives with the division. Founder Brand is enterprise-integrated but not a JM1 operating division.
