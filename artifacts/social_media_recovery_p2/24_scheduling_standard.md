# Scheduling Standard

Timezone: America/New_York. Schedule only approved content with rights/compliance/privacy gates passed. Avoid duplicate posts, same-hour brand bunching, and same-day urgency unless a human owner approves. Failed posts return to Draft/Review and require post ID/error capture.

## Platform Routing

For standard paired Meta brands, use Meta Business Suite for supported Facebook and Instagram scheduling.
