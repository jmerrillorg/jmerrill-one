## Asset Rules

- Do not use personal/private images without explicit permission.
- Do not use donor, volunteer, client, recipient, author, or family stories without approval.
- Do not imply legal, financial, charitable, or publishing outcomes that are not verified.
- Productions may serve as the enterprise media creation layer for other brands, but content ownership remains with the brand owner.
- Social derivatives must reference asset approval status before scheduling.
