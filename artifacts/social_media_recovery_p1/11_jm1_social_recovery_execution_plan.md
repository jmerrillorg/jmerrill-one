- Enterprise repository/location selected.
- Social Channel Registry specification created.
- Profile remediation matrix created.

- Content object and derivative specs created.
- Content lifecycle and approval model created.
- 30-day recovery calendar drafted.

- Attribution model created.
- Founder decision packet created.

## Phase 2: Founder Review

Required decisions:

- Approve public address policy: "Headquartered in Columbus, Ohio."
- Approve proposed profile values or edits for each brand.
- Confirm current canonical URLs, public emails, phone numbers, and CTAs.
- Confirm Financial compliance language and non-attorney boundaries.
- Confirm Foundation privacy/mission language.
- Confirm Productions identity reset from legacy Spotlight references.
- Approve the first 30-day recovery cadence.
- Approve Meta Business Suite as the immediate execution surface for Facebook/Instagram.

## Phase 3: Admin Audit

Inspect the J Merrill One Meta Business Portfolio before any changes:

- Facebook Pages
- connected Instagram accounts
- Business Portfolio ownership
- Page IDs
- Instagram account IDs
- admin/full-control assignments
- partial-access assignments
- connected ad accounts
- pixels/datasets
- domains
- existing integrations
- current page/Instagram pairings
- duplicate or legacy business portfolios
- orphaned assets

Also confirm LinkedIn and YouTube account ownership, admin roles, profile URLs, and verification states.

## Phase 4: Profile Remediation

After founder approval and admin audit:

- Correct inaccurate bios/about text.
- Replace visible street addresses with approved public address language where platform rules permit.
- Correct Foundation operating-hours/status presentation.
- Correct Productions legacy identity and website link.
- Modernize Publishing author-acquisition profile language.
- Reposition Financial profile toward planning and readiness with compliance safeguards.
- Confirm profile/cover imagery.
- Record before/after evidence.

## Phase 5: Content Recovery

After profile remediation approval:

- Build first 20 canonical content objects.
- Generate platform derivatives.
- Review derivatives against canon, compliance, and asset rights.
- Schedule approved Facebook/Instagram content in Meta Business Suite.
- Publish LinkedIn/YouTube content through native surfaces only after ownership confirmation.
- Capture engagement and conversion evidence manually until registry automation is authorized.

## Phase 6: Parallel Marketing System Build

Do not block live recovery on automation. In parallel, prepare later authorization for:

- Dataverse content registry
- approval evidence workflow
- campaign metadata
- attribution records
- Power Apps views
- Power Automate internal notifications/handoffs
- Customer Insights journeys for consented lifecycle communications
- governed API/connectors only where platform, DLP, and business value justify them

## Stop Conditions

Stop only for:

- missing canon value that would require invention
- destructive historical/profile/schema/admin change
- materially new architecture decision outside this P1 canon
- new licensing or spend requirement
- inability to verify admin ownership for a required channel
