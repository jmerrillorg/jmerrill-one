# JM1 Brand Editorial Missions

Status: CANON-CANDIDATE

## Shared Editorial Rules

- Human-First: begin with the person, need, question, problem, opportunity, or impact.
- Why-First: explain why the topic matters before presenting a service.
- Canonical facts outrank derivative copy.
- Social content may be lighter and more conversational, but it must not invent claims, credentials, outcomes, testimonials, or legal/compliance meaning.
- Content should invite next action without pressuring or confusing the audience.

## J Merrill One

Mission: Build what lasts.

J Merrill One content should express enterprise authority, founder perspective, business-building, legacy, leadership, technology, AI and automation, Microsoft ecosystem implementation, behind-JM1 progress, division stories, community, and cross-division milestones.

Primary outcome: trust in JM1 as a serious, human-first enterprise builder.

## J Merrill Publishing

Mission: Help authors move from manuscript uncertainty to publishable, marketable books.

Pillars:

- Authors: introductions, stories, milestones, testimonials, interviews.
- Books: releases, covers, excerpts where rights permit, launch announcements, genre highlights.
- Publishing education: manuscript readiness, editing, covers, ISBNs, distribution, royalties, audiobooks, publishing myths, author rights.
- Invitation: manuscript submission, publishing consultation, inquiry/intake, educational CTA.

Primary outcome: qualified author acquisition.

## J Merrill Financial

Mission: Help families and organizations prepare, organize, and protect what matters.

Pillars:

- Know: education and awareness.
- Prepare: checklists and readiness questions.
- Protect: family and estate scenarios.
- Plan: planning services and next actions.
- Maintain: LegacyCare, reviews, updates, and ongoing planning.

Allowed topics include wills, trusts, powers of attorney, healthcare directives, executors, beneficiaries, minor children, blended families, probate, digital assets, document organization, funeral/pre-need readiness, business succession, long-term care, and legacy planning.

Primary outcome: qualified planning conversations and appointments.

Control: Financial content must maintain non-attorney positioning and must not provide unauthorized legal advice.

## J Merrill Foundation

Mission: Move from need to human story to action to measurable impact.

Architecture:

Need -> Human Story -> Action -> People -> Impact -> Invitation

Pillars:

- community need
- programs
- volunteers
- donors
- partners
- outcomes
- impact
- campaigns
- behind-the-scenes service
- invitations to participate

Primary outcome: donor, volunteer, partner, and community engagement.

## J Merrill Productions

Mission: Make stories visible, useful, and moving across JM1 and external client work.

Pillars:

- Watch: finished work.
- Behind the Lens: production process.
- Story: why the project mattered.
- Create: production education.
- Work With Us: client acquisition.

Primary outcomes: production leads, portfolio growth, and cross-enterprise media amplification.
