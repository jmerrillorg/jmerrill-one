# JM1 Content Lifecycle and Approval

Status: CANON-CANDIDATE

## Lifecycle

IDEA -> CANONICAL DRAFT -> SOURCE VERIFIED -> BRAND REVIEW -> COMPLIANCE REVIEW -> APPROVED -> DERIVATIVES GENERATED -> DERIVATIVES REVIEWED -> SCHEDULED -> PUBLISHED -> ENGAGEMENT CAPTURED -> ANALYZED -> REUSED / RETIRED

## Approval Model

Founder approval remains required for:

- major brand-positioning changes
- sensitive public statements
- new public claims
- legal/compliance-sensitive Financial content
- crisis communications
- significant enterprise announcements
- content materially changing JM1 canon

Routine derivatives may later become eligible for delegated approval after a pattern has been approved and measured.

## Publishing Execution

Meta Business Suite is the approved channel-native execution surface for Facebook and Instagram scheduling, publishing, Reels, Stories where supported, inbox/community management, native analytics, page/account administration, and campaign/ad execution when separately authorized.

LinkedIn and YouTube may use native/API execution surfaces after their account ownership and compliance rules are confirmed.

## Microsoft Ownership Boundary

Microsoft remains the enterprise system for:

- canonical content records
- approval evidence
- customer/audience records
- consent
- lead and opportunity management
- Customer Insights journeys
- attribution
- analytics
- AI orchestration
- enterprise reporting

Customer Insights - Journeys must not be forced into the role of Facebook/Instagram publishing engine.

## Automation Progression

Manual -> AI-Assisted -> Human-Approved Automation -> Policy-Governed Low-Risk Automation

During P1:

- No autonomous publishing.
- No automatic profile edits.
- No silent AI fact changes.
- No new Dataverse table/Power Automate implementation without later authorization.
