# JM1 Microsoft Native Capability Activation Standard v1

Status: CANON - v1.0

Founder & CEO approval recorded by CV-01 on 2026-08-13T02:54:55Z.

## Purpose

JM1 uses Microsoft-native capabilities before building custom substitutes when the Microsoft capability fits the business need.

## Activation Rules

Verify actual tenant capability state before implementation. Prove new shared capabilities in governed nonproduction with synthetic data before production activation. Do not purchase licensing or capacity without explicit Founder approval.

## Privacy and Consent

Enterprise Contact identity does not imply enterprise-wide visibility of responses, journeys, or sensitive division context. Website intake is not blanket marketing consent. Consent must preserve purpose/topic where applicable, channel, source, timestamp, opt-out, and suppression.

## Ownership

One owns enterprise Microsoft-native patterns, governance, reusable contracts, and cross-division boundaries. Divisions own domain-specific implementation and business meaning. Cross-repository handoff is required when a proof belongs in a division repository.

## Customer Voice

Customer Voice owns Microsoft-native survey creation, response collection, structured feedback, native response analytics, and Dataverse-linked responses. Domain systems own relationship/program context and consequential follow-up.

## Customer Insights - Journeys

Journeys owns lifecycle, nurture, onboarding, event, stewardship, and campaign communications where consent and context support that use. It does not automatically own sensitive assistance, regulated/advisory, service-resolution, one-off human, or HITL communications.

## Power Automate Boundary

Power Automate owns bounded operational workflow, internal notification, deterministic record-triggered automation, and system integration. It must not become a custom journey/nurture engine when Journeys fits.

## No-Duplicate Rule

Do not create duplicate Contact identity, second consent model, duplicate response store, custom survey platform, or custom campaign engine.

## Production Criteria

Production activation requires tenant state evidence, nonproduction proof, privacy/consent validation, DLP review, rollback/disable plan, and regression validation.
